Hi Cy,

I'd rather write you a letter. This way you can keep it and read it again whenever you want.

We haven't had much time together. I met you once in person, and the rest was Tuesday nights on a screen. So I don't have a long list of stories. What I have is what I saw in the short time I knew you.

There are times when life is too busy challenging us, but I was proud of you for choosing to stay grounded. In every giant you face, you always choose to know God more, to experience Him, and to let Him guide your life, even while everything else felt shaky. I'm proud of you.

You're strong, and you're also soft-hearted. Sometimes, your tears may fall easily from your eyes, and I hope you never feel bad about that. You cry and you keep going. That has been true of you the whole time, and your faith has grown so much. It shows.

Going home doesn't mean leaving any of this behind. God is in your province too. He was already there before you arrived, and He is the same One who has been holding you here.

I'm praying that He provides for everything you need, that your work goes well, and that you find a church family close to home. If you ever want to tell us how you're doing, we're only a message away.

I'm sorry I couldn't be there on Tuesdays lately. Night shifts took me away, but I've been cheering for you the whole time.

Go well, Cy. Keep growing, keep trusting, and keep being the girl who lets God make her brave.

With love and prayers,

Your sister in Christ, Ate Grace
